Hi, I'm Lisa!

I'm a lifelong animal lover and former veterinary technician who believes pets deserve dependable, patient and loving care when their people can't be there.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Pet care has always felt natural to me

Before starting Suncoast & Paws, I worked as a veterinary technician where I gained hands-on experience caring for pets with all kinds of personalities and needs.

Now I get to bring that experience into pet sitting and dog walking while giving local families someone they can feel comfortable trusting with their pets and their homes.

My goal is simple: keep your pet's routine as familiar as possible, give them plenty of attention and make sure you never have to wonder how they're doing.

WHAT MATTERS TO ME

Care that's personal and dependable

Every pet is different, so I try to make every visit feel comfortable, familiar and tailored to them.

Lots of Love

Individual Care

I follow your pet's normal routine and adjust care based on their personality and comfort level.

Peace of Mind

Regular photos and updates help you stay connected and know your pet is happy and cared for.
